# Source Review Guide

Reviewers confirm identity, provenance, edition, author, category, marja and
currentness separately from technical extraction quality. Technical gates can
reject corrupted passages, but they cannot decide religious authority.

For fiqh, specify the marja when a ruling depends on it. Keep historical
material distinct from current rulings. Reviewers may approve a source while
keeping individual rejected chunks excluded; approval never restores rejected
evidence.

Required review evidence includes source hash, page/section provenance,
representative beginning/middle/end samples, corruption report, and a clear
decision. Unknown fields remain unknown until verified.
